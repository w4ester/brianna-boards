Meet Nani

The story behind the treats & boards — a passion for bringing people together through beautiful, delicious food.

My Story

Hi, I'm Brianna — but everyone calls me Nani! My love for creating started with family gatherings where the appetizer table was always the heart of the party. I discovered that beautifully presented food isn't just delicious — it's a conversation starter, a work of art, and a way to show love.

What began as a hobby for hosting friends and family has grown into a passion for crafting boards and sweet treats that make every occasion special. From charcuterie boards to macarons, cake pops, and bento cakes — each creation is made with love and arranged with care.

My Philosophy

I believe that great food should be accessible, beautiful, and bring joy. That's why I focus on using quality ingredients that taste as good as they look, while keeping everything affordable for everyday celebrations.

Every creation tells a story — from the selection of artisan cheeses on a charcuterie board to the delicate flavors of a macaron. I want your order to be the centerpiece that gets everyone talking.
